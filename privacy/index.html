Privacy

The studio site · Last updated 20 October 2026

This page covers oatmealmorning.com, the studio's front page. Each app has its own privacy policy: OCD: Off, Closed, Done and SK8.

Anonymous visit counts

We count page views and which app card was tapped, so we know whether anyone visits. For this we use PostHog, an analytics service (PostHog Inc., data stored in the United States). PostHog records a random identifier in your browser's local storage, the type of device and browser, and an approximate location derived from your IP address. We do not use cookies, we do not record your screen, and we do not track you across other websites. You can block this with any content blocker; the site works the same without it.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log the IP address of visitors. See GitHub's privacy statement.

What we don't do

We do not sell or share your information, show ads, or ask for your name, email, or anything else. There are no accounts and no forms.

Contact

Questions? Write to oatmealmorningstudio@gmail.com.
